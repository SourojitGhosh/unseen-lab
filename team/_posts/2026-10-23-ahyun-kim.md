Ahyun Kim is an undergraduate researcher with the UNSEEN Lab, majoring in Statistics and Analytics with minors in Media and Journalism and Data Science. Her research interests include human-computer interaction and user experience, particularly how people perceive and engage with generative AI and the content it creates. She is especially interested in using mixed methods to understand these interactions and their ethical implications.
