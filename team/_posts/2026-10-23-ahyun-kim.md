Ahyun Kim is an undergraduate researcher in the UNSEEN Lab, majoring in Statistics and Analytics at UNC-Chapel Hill with minors in Media and Journalism (Advertising/Public Relations track) and Data Science. Her interests include human-computer interaction and user experience, particularly how people perceive and engage with generative AI and the content it creates. She is also interested in using mixed methods to understand these interactions, as well as their ethical implications and potential risks.
