Vladimir Martirosyan is a first-year PhD student at the UNC-Chapel Hill School of Data and Information Sciences (SDIS) and a graduate researcher in the UNSEEN Lab, advised by Professor Francesca Tripodi. He holds a BS in Data Science with a Certificate in Economic Analytics from UW-Madison and an MS in Information Systems from UMD-College Park. His interests include misinformation, algorithmic feeds, recommender systems, and digital platform governance. He is also interested in combining game-theoretic modeling with mixed methods to examine interactions between humans and AI agents online.
