Shreya Ravi is an Undergraduate Researcher for the UNSEEN Lab, pursuing a double major in Computer Science and Music. Her research interests include analyzing the impact that Artificial Intelligence has on creative industries, specifically music. She is especially interested in exploring the ethical and societal implications of AI, including how many minority communities within the creative industry are impacted by its growing influence.
